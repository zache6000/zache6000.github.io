FAQ

How do I order?
Add your pieces to the bag, fill in your details at checkout and send the ready-made order message to us. We confirm everything with you by message before shipping.
How do I pay and how much is delivery?
Payment and the delivery fee for your city are confirmed with you directly when we reply to your order.
Which size should I take?
Every hoodie is cut oversized. Take your usual size for the relaxed look, or one size down for a cleaner fit. Size M measures 60 cm across the chest and 68 cm long. Need another size's measurements? Message us.
How do I care for the print?
Wash inside out at 30°C, don't iron directly on the print and don't tumble dry.
Will there be restocks?
Drops are limited. Follow us on Instagram to hear first about restocks and Drop 03.
